# SmartScreen Privacy Suite 1.0.0

Local-first webcam privacy for Windows 10/11. It recognizes you, notices when someone else may be
looking at your screen, and hides your content or locks Windows. CPU-only, offline, no account.

## Install
1. Download `SmartScreen Privacy Suite Setup 1.0.0.exe` below and check its SHA-256 (listed below).
2. Run it. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows may warn: choose "More info" → "Run anyway".
3. Enroll your face (five samples), then turn protection on.

## Highlights
- Owner and trusted-people recognition (OpenCV YuNet + SFace), fully on-device
- Fail-closed privacy curtain: camera or AI problems never reveal the screen
- Blur / dim / pixelate / curtain, all driven by one Privacy Intensity slider
- Walk-away lock, escalation timers, profiles with Wi-Fi auto-switch
- Event history that explains every protection decision
- Optional security images of unrecognized people: off by default, encrypted, newest 30 kept

## Known limits
- Recognition is a similarity threshold, not proof of identity. Liveness is a heuristic, not
  anti-spoofing: a tilted photo, a replayed video or a mask can pass. On the test laptop only head
  movement (not blinking) triggered liveness, so you may need to move slightly before the screen
  is revealed.
- Up to 250 ms after switching virtual desktops the overlay may not yet be on the new desktop.
- SmartScreen is a privacy aid, not a security boundary: a local administrator can stop it. It never
  unlocks Windows.
